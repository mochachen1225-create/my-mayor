# Newgrounds 上架資料

> Newgrounds 社群對 AI 生成圖很反感（美術區明文禁止 AI 圖），所以 **Newgrounds 版不放 AI 插畫**，改用原本的 SVG 頭像。宣傳圖也一律用沒有 AI 插畫的版本。

## 要上傳的檔案
| 項目 | 檔案 |
|---|---|
| 遊戲 | `scripts/build-newgrounds.sh` 打包出來的 `my-mayor-newgrounds.zip`（`index.html` 在 zip 最外層，否則 Newgrounds 會跳 403） |
| 圖示（Icon） | **Newgrounds 的圖示是橫的：用 `promo/newgrounds/newgrounds-icon-liver-wide-1920x1080.png`**；正方形版 `newgrounds-icon-liver-512.png`（手繪「爆肝的肝」，另有 1024 版；另一款：`newgrounds-icon-cat-512.png` 手繪躺平貓；備用：`newgrounds-icon-512.png` SVG 頭像版） |
| 橫幅 | `promo/newgrounds/banner-cat-2400x1260.png`（躺平貓，可放頁面或 Devlog） |
| 截圖 | `promo/newgrounds/newgrounds-screenshot-1~3.png` |
| 宣傳影片 | ⚠️ 不要用 `promo/` 裡的試玩廣告，裡面有 AI 插畫 |

## 頁面介紹（英文）

**You're the campaign manager. Your boss is random. Your liver is not.**

Four months until Election Day in N City, a fictional Taiwanese metropolis. Your candidate was drawn at random, doesn't always listen, and the internet has an opinion about everything you do.

- 🔥 Put out fires: rats on the evening news, typhoon day-off decisions, an intern posting from the wrong account. You have 20 seconds.
- 🏃 Shake hands at the morning market (mini-game). Grandmas count triple.
- 📺 Election night with a live count, and the occasional comeback.
- 58 endings: 43 for your candidate, 15 for you.

Everything is fictional and it pokes fun at campaign culture on all sides. Taiwan's real local elections are on November 28. A short primer on the title screen explains the basics.

⏱ About 10 minutes · 🐢 Take-It-Easy mode removes all timers · 🌏 English / 繁體中文
